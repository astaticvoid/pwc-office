Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 4, 2026

The Pray Without Ceasing Daily Office app respects your privacy. Our principles are simple:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell your personal data.
  • Local Storage Only: All of your app preferences (such as text size, theme, and liturgical options) are saved locally on your device. They are never sent to our servers.
  • No Tracking Cookies: We do not use third-party trackers or invasive advertising cookies.

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact the developer via the official repository or synod contacts.
