Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 4, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Pray Without Ceasing app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices and the web that was created as an open-source project. This service is provided "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII). The Application operates entirely on your local device. Any preferences you configure within the Application (such as display themes or text sizes) are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to external servers.

Third Party Access

Since the Application does not collect any personal data, no data is shared with third parties. The Application does not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising networks, or cross-site tracking cookies.

Security

We are concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. Because the Application does not transmit or store personal data remotely, your data remains secure on your device. We rely on the security mechanisms provided by your device's operating system to protect locally stored preferences.

Children's Privacy

The Application does not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected, the Application complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.

Contact Us
